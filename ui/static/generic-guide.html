Generic Definitions Guide

How to add custom activities, recipes, services, and items before the wiki catches up with new game content.

🏃

Activities

Select Generic from the activity dropdown in Column 3 to open the activity form. Fill in the name, skill, location, max efficiency (copy the number shown in-game — if it says "180%", enter 180), and required skill level.

Then choose how you want to enter the steps:

Observed mode

Enter the steps shown in-game with no gear equipped. The tool reverse-calculates the true base steps, accounting for your character level and collectibles.

Known Base mode

Enter the true base steps directly if you already know them (e.g. copied from another player or the patch notes).

Ambiguous result? If the reverse calculation can't pin down a unique value, you'll see a Calibrate button. It suggests a specific gearset to equip in-game — enter the steps you see with that gear and the tool will solve for the exact base value.
⚒️

Recipes

Same as activities — select Generic from the recipe dropdown. You also pick a service (use an existing wiki service, or create a generic one — see below). The reverse calculator accounts for service bonuses automatically.

The XP input follows the same Observed / Known Base toggle as steps.

🏭

Services

Inside the recipe form, choose Generic Service from the service dropdown. Give it a name, pick the skill category and location, then add stat rows (WE, DA, DR, etc.) with their values.

Generic services can be saved and reused across multiple recipes.

🔧

Custom Items Equipment

Click 🔧 Create Generic Item in any gear slot popup, or find the Generic Items section at the bottom of Column 1. The form lets you configure:

  • Icon — pick any emoji, with an optional color tint
  • Slot — head, chest, tool, ring, and all other equipment slots
  • Keywords — for uniqueness rules like map or light source
  • Stats — add rows with skill, location, stat type, and value
  • Crafted toggle — enables quality tabs (Normal → Eternal) so each tier can have different stats
  • Rarity — sets the color border on the item icon

Generic items appear in gear slot pickers filtered by slot, show up in Column 1 with checkboxes, and are included in optimizer runs just like wiki items.

🧪

Consumables & Collectibles

In the item form, selecting the Consumable slot switches to Normal/Fine quality tabs. Selecting Collectible strips the form down to just name, icon, and stats — no slot or rarity needed.

💾

Saving & Sharing

Every form has a Name field and a Save button. Saved definitions appear in a Custom group at the top of their respective dropdowns.

Check Share with community to make a definition visible to other users — they can clone it to their own session. You can edit or delete your saved definitions at any time.

Community definitions show up in a separate group in the dropdown. Cloning one creates a private copy under your session that you can freely edit.
